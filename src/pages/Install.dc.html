Honest advice

Repair or replace?
We'll tell you straight.

One customer was quoted for a new door. We restored the old one instead — and saved her about $5,000.

Repair it if…

  • It drags, grinds or jumps the track
  • The lock or handle is broken
  • The track is worn but the frame is solid
  • One glass panel is cracked
  • The screen is torn or bent

Replace it if…

  • The frame itself is corroded through
  • You're remodeling or changing the opening
  • You want a different door style
  • Parts are no longer available
