How much does it cost to replace sliding glass door glass?

+

For one panel: about $550–$700 for single-pane tempered glass, $1,200–$1,500 for double-pane insulated glass, and from about $1,800 for impact glass. That includes the glass, removing and hauling away the old glass, and installation. We confirm the exact price on site before ordering.

Can fogged double-pane glass be fixed without replacing it?

+

Not reliably. Fog means the seal has failed and moisture is inside the sealed unit. Defogging services can clear it for a while, but the seal stays broken. Replacing the insulated glass unit is the lasting fix, and your frame stays in place.

Do I need a new door if the glass breaks?

+

Usually not. If the frame and track are in good shape, we replace only the damaged panel’s glass. A whole new sliding door typically costs several thousand dollars, so a glass replacement is far cheaper.

Can you replace just one panel?

+

Yes. You only pay for the panel that’s damaged. If the other panel is fogging too, we can quote both so they match.

How long does it take?

+

Single-pane tempered glass is often done the same or next day. Double-pane and impact glass are made to order, so we measure first, order, and come back to install. We give you the lead time with your quote.

My door has impact glass. Can you use regular glass?

+

No. Impact doors need impact-rated laminated glass so the door keeps its hurricane rating. We check the glass type before ordering.

What should I do until it’s replaced?

+

Keep kids and pets away, tape over cracks with packing tape, and slide the door gently or keep it locked. If the glass shattered, call us — we can remove the broken glass safely.

Is there a charge to come out?

+

No. The service call is free when we do the repair, and you see the exact price before any work starts.
