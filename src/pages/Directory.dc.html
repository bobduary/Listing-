Home/How we rank

How we rank sliding door pros

“Best match” is our default order. Here’s exactly what goes into it, and what doesn’t.

What moves a company up

  • Homeowner ratings and how many reviews
    A 4.8 from 200 reviews counts for more than a 5.0 from 3.
  • Honest pricing
    How often homeowners report paying within the company’s posted prices.
  • Business verified
    Companies that passed our checks rank higher than ones we haven’t checked.
  • The right specialty
    A company that specializes in your repair ranks above one that only offers it.
  • Fast replies
    How quickly they usually answer.
  • Distance
    Closer companies rank higher, and we only show companies that serve your area.

What can’t be bought

No company can pay for a better rating, a badge or a higher spot in the ranking. A company with a free listing can rank first.

Promoted spots are ads. Up to 2 can appear above the ranked list, always labeled “Promoted” and outlined so they’re easy to tell apart. They must serve your area too.

Prefer another order?

On the pros list you can sort by rating, price, distance or fastest reply instead.

What our badges mean

Every profile shows what we checked and when. A badge means we checked those facts. It doesn’t mean we guarantee the company’s work.

Listed

Business verified

Our team checked:

  • {{ v.label }}: {{ v.how }}
badgeLicensed installer

For companies that do installation themselves, not through a subcontractor. We looked up their Florida license on DBPR (the state licensing board) and it was active. Repair-only work often doesn’t need a state license, so repair companies don’t need this badge.

What we don’t check

We don’t check insurance, and we don’t guarantee anyone’s work. Before you hire, ask to see their insurance certificate and get the price and warranty in writing. Details like warranty, hours and call-out fees are reported by the company and labeled that way.

Home/Ownership disclosure

Who owns FixMySlide

FixMySlide has the same owner as {{ ownerCompanies }}, a sliding door repair company. We want you to know that up front.

How we keep it fair

  • Clearly labeled. Wherever {{ ownerCompanies }} appears, it’s marked “Same owner as FixMySlide”.
  • No ranking advantage. It’s ranked by exactly the same published rules as every other company, and it’s never shown in a promoted spot for free.
  • Same checks, same badges. It gets a badge only by passing the same checks as everyone else.
  • Reviews we don’t touch. Reviews come only from homeowners who report a job with proof. We don’t remove or edit honest reviews of any company, including competitors.
  • You choose. You call or text companies directly. We don’t route your call to anyone.
