Before you hire: 4 questions to ask

  1. What's your price range for this repair? Compare it to the fair price above.
  2. Is there a call-out fee, and is it waived if I hire you?
  3. What warranty do you give on parts and labor? 90 days is common; a year is better.
  4. Are you insured? Every pro on SlideRepairFL is, and we check with the insurer.
